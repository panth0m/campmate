Best Camping Chairs in Australia

The best camping chair is not always the most expensive one. The right pick depends on whether you want roomy comfort for car camping or a light packed size for touring.

What to compare

  • Seat height: lower chairs pack smaller but are harder to get out of.
  • Weight: premium lightweight chairs cost more but travel better.
  • Durability: check fabric, frame design and realistic load limits.
