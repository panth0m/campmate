Coleman vs OZtrail Tents: Which Is Better for Australian Campers?

Compare Coleman and OZtrail camping tents for Australia. Prices, quality, warranty and where each brand makes more sense.

Brand overview

Coleman is a globally recognised American brand with over 120 years of camping heritage. OZtrail is an Australian brand designed specifically for local conditions and distributed widely through BCF, Anaconda and Big W. Both are widely available and target family campers.

Price range

Coleman and OZtrail tents generally sit in the $150–$800 range. OZtrail tends to be slightly cheaper at the entry level, while Coleman has stronger mid-range options like the Instant Up Gold series. At the top end, both brands offer full-feature family tents over $600.

Setup and features

Coleman's Instant Up range is one of the best-known fast-setup tents in Australia — genuinely up in under 2 minutes for most people. OZtrail's FastFrame series aims for similar convenience. Both brands offer dark-room technology to reduce morning heat and light penetration.

Build quality and warranty

Coleman offers a solid warranty and consistent build quality across its range. OZtrail's quality can vary more between product lines — their premium options are competitive, but budget tents cut more corners. Both brands are well-supported at Australian retailers.

Which should you choose?

Choose Coleman for the Instant Up convenience and reliable build quality, especially if you want a tent from $400–$700 that sets up fast and lasts multiple seasons. Choose OZtrail if you want a wider range of size options at a lower price point, or if you're buying from BCF or Anaconda where OZtrail stock is always strong.
