Best Down Sleeping Bags for Australian Camping in 2026

The best down sleeping bags in Australia reviewed. Sea to Summit, Enlightened Equipment, Western Mountaineering and more compared for warmth, weight and value.

Down vs synthetic: the core decision

Down sleeping bags are lighter and more compressible than synthetic at equivalent warmth ratings. They last longer when cared for. The downside: they lose insulation when wet and take longer to dry. Synthetic bags are heavier but perform better when damp and dry faster — better for wet coastal or alpine camping.

Understanding temperature ratings

The EU EN 13537 standard gives bags a Comfort, Limit and Extreme rating. The Comfort rating is what most people should use as a guide — it reflects the temperature at which a cold sleeper can sleep comfortably. Don't rely on the Extreme rating for normal camping. In practice, buy a bag rated 5°C colder than the coldest night you expect.

Fill power: what the number means

Fill power (e.g. 650fp, 850fp) measures how much loft 1 oz of down creates. Higher fill power = lighter and more packable for the same warmth. 600–650fp is good quality; 750fp+ is premium; 900fp+ is ultralight expedition quality. Higher fill power bags cost significantly more.

Best down sleeping bags in Australia

Sea to Summit's Spark series offers excellent warmth-to-weight for Australian conditions. Enlightened Equipment's Revelation quilts are popular with ultralight hikers. Western Mountaineering is the premium benchmark for serious alpine use. For most campers, Therm-a-Rest and Nemo offer excellent quality at more accessible price points.

Caring for a down sleeping bag

Store down bags loosely (never compressed long-term) to maintain loft. Wash with down-specific detergent on a gentle cycle with tumble drying on low with tennis balls to break up clumps. Compress only when travelling. With proper care, a good down bag lasts 15–20+ years.
