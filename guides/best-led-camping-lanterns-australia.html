Best LED Camping Lanterns in Australia for 2026

The best LED camping lanterns in Australia reviewed. Rechargeable, solar and battery-powered options compared by brightness, runtime and value.

Why LED lanterns are now the best choice

LED lanterns have improved dramatically in recent years. Modern rechargeable LEDs last 40–100+ hours per charge, produce clean bright light, and many now charge via USB-C — meaning you can top them up from a power bank, solar panel or car. They've largely replaced gas lanterns for most campers.

Brightness: what lumen count do you need?

For a small tent interior or intimate campsite, 200–300 lumens is plenty. For cooking, group activities or lighting a large awning area, 600–1000 lumens is more useful. Most quality lanterns have adjustable brightness so you can run low for long runtime and high when you need it.

Best rechargeable lanterns

Goal Zero Lighthouse 400 and BioLite BaseCharge are premium options with long runtimes and USB charging ports to charge other devices. Fenix and Nitecore make extremely reliable LED lanterns used by serious campers. For mainstream use, the Coleman LED Lantern range offers excellent brightness-per-dollar.

Solar-charging lanterns

For extended trips where you can't easily recharge, solar-integrated lanterns like the Goal Zero Lighthouse Micro and BioLite SunLight 100 are genuinely useful. They won't fully recharge from a couple of hours of weak sun, but top up enough for extended trips in good Australian sunlight.

Battery-powered vs rechargeable

Rechargeable lanterns are more convenient for most camping. AA/D-cell battery lanterns have an advantage in genuine remote travel where charging isn't possible — you can buy batteries anywhere. For most Australian car camping, rechargeable USB lanterns are the better default choice.

Top picks

Best overall: Goal Zero Lighthouse 400. Best value: Coleman Battery LED Lantern. Best solar: BioLite SunLight 100. Best premium: Fenix CL26R Pro. Best budget rechargeable: Generic 500lm USB lantern from BCF or Anaconda.
