Best Camping Sleeping Mats for Australia in 2026

The best camping sleeping mats and pads reviewed for Australia. Inflatable, self-inflating and foam mats compared for warmth, comfort and packability.

Why your sleeping mat matters more than your sleeping bag

Ground cold kills warmth faster than air cold. A tent floor in winter can strip heat from your sleeping bag much faster than the air temperature suggests. Your sleeping mat is actually the more critical insulation layer below 10°C — its R-value (thermal resistance) is what protects you from cold ground.

R-value explained

R-value measures thermal resistance. An R-1 mat is minimal (summer only). R-2 is a 3-season baseline. R-4+ is suitable for cold to alpine conditions. For most Australian camping (not alpine), an R-2 to R-3 mat covers all but the coldest nights. Stack two mats for extreme cold.

Inflatable sleeping mats

Inflatable mats offer the best warmth-to-weight and packability. They compress small and are the preferred choice for hikers and anyone minimising pack weight. Therm-a-Rest NeoAir and Sea to Summit Ether Light XT are benchmarks. Downside: puncture risk — always carry a repair kit.

Self-inflating mats

Self-inflating mats (foam plus air) are more puncture-resistant and easier to use — open the valve and they inflate most of the way automatically. They're heavier and bulkier than fully inflatable mats. Good for car camping, where bulk is less critical. Therm-a-Rest ProLite and Exped SIM Comfort are solid options.

Foam sleeping mats

Closed-cell foam mats are indestructible, lightweight and cheap. They don't inflate, so they're bulky to transport (usually strapped outside a pack). R-value is modest. They're excellent as a backup layer, for sit pads, or for conditions where puncture risk is high. Therm-a-Rest Z Lite is the benchmark.

Top picks

Best inflatable: Therm-a-Rest NeoAir XLite. Best self-inflating: Therm-a-Rest ProLite. Best budget: Klymit Static V. Best for car camping comfort: Exped MegaMat. Best ultralight: Sea to Summit Ether Light XT Extreme.
