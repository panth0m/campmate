Best 2-Burner Camping Stoves in Australia for 2026

The best 2-burner camping stoves in Australia reviewed. Coleman, Camp Chef, Gasmate and more compared by heat output, portability and value.

Why a 2-burner stove for camping?

A 2-burner stove is the workhorse of family and group car camping. You can cook bacon and eggs at the same time, boil water while frying onions, or handle any camp breakfast without juggling a single burner. For base camp setups, they're nearly essential.

What to look for in a 2-burner stove

Key specs are total heat output (measured in BTU or MJ/h — more is better for fast cooking), burner spacing (wider is easier to use with big pans), wind resistance, and fuel type (most use LPG canister or propane). Folding leg designs are more portable; some models include a lid that doubles as a windshield.

Coleman 2-burner stoves

Coleman's Classic and Even-Heat 2-burner stoves are the benchmark for value in Australia. They're reliable, widely stocked, and heat output is solid for standard camp cooking. The Even-Heat model distributes heat more evenly across larger pans — useful for pancakes and frying.

Camp Chef Explorer and Everest

Camp Chef makes some of the best-performing 2-burner stoves available in Australia. The Explorer's independent burners can reach very high output, and the design supports optional accessories like griddles and BBQ plates. Premium price, premium results.

Gasmate and Australian brands

Gasmate is an Australian brand with a strong following for straightforward, robust stoves at mid-range prices. Their Platinum 2-burner is a popular choice for families who want reliable performance without paying premium import prices.

Top picks

Best overall: Camp Chef Everest 2X. Best value: Coleman Classic 2-Burner. Best Australian brand: Gasmate Platinum Portable. Best for groups: Camp Chef Explorer 2X with griddle.
