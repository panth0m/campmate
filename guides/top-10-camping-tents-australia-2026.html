How we ranked these

CampMate ranks camping tents based on buyer ratings, review volume and value for money across Australian retailers. We look for products that consistently perform well for Australian conditions — not just specs on paper.

Australian buying tip

Australian summers can be intense — look for tents with strong ventilation and UV-rated fabrics. In cooler months, a tent with a good hydrostatic head rating (2000mm+) will handle rain comfortably.
