Best Coolers in Australia

Cooler and ice box picks for family trips, weekend camps and simple food storage.

What matters most

For many campers, cooler choice comes down to capacity, ice retention and how far the box needs to be moved. CampMate focuses on proven hard coolers, practical wheeled options and compact sizes that suit real camping use.

Rather than chasing a single “best” product, CampMate looks for options that match common Australian camping use cases: family weekends, touring, beach trips, cooler nights and simple car-camping setups.

How to shortlist well

  • Start with the size, comfort or output you really need, not the biggest spec on the page.
  • Use CampMate product pages to compare a handful of realistic options instead of dozens of random listings.
  • Open retailer searches only after you have narrowed the field to a few products that match your trip style.
