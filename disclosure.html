Affiliate Disclosure

CampMate Australia uses affiliate links and participates in affiliate and advertising programs. This means we may earn a commission when you click certain links on this site and then make a purchase. This never increases the price you pay.

Programs we work with

  • Amazon Associates - as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases made through Amazon links.
  • Commission Factory - we partner with Australian retailers such as BCF, Anaconda, Snowys, Tentworld and Wild Earth through the Commission Factory network.
  • Other retailer and marketplace affiliate programs that help fund the site.

How it works

When you click a store button on a product or comparison page, you may be taken to a retailer through a tracked affiliate link. If you buy something during that visit, the retailer may pay CampMate a small referral commission. You pay exactly the same price you would pay by going to the store directly.

Our commitment

Affiliate relationships never decide our rankings, ratings or recommendations. We show multiple retailer options for each product so you can choose the store and price that suit you best. Reference prices shown on CampMate can change at any time, so always confirm the final price on the retailer's website before you buy.

If you have any questions about how we use affiliate links, please contact us.
