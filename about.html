About CampMate Australia

CampMate is an Australia-focused camping gear site built to help shoppers move from early research into simple comparison and click-through decisions.

How the site is meant to work

  • Start with a guide page for category-level recommendations.
  • Open a product page for a short summary, current marketplace pricing and quick store links.
  • Click through to a trusted listing when you are ready to buy.

What CampMate covers

The site currently focuses on tents, chairs, coolers, stoves, lanterns and sleep systems for common camping use cases in Australia.
