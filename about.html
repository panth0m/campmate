About CampMate Australia

CampMate Australia is an independent camping gear comparison website built for Australian outdoor shoppers. We bring together tents, camp chairs, coolers, portable fridges, stoves, lanterns and sleep systems so you can compare specifications, reference prices and retailer options in one place before you buy.

The idea started with a simple frustration: comparing camping gear across Australian retailers usually meant opening a dozen browser tabs. CampMate pulls that information into clear product pages and buying guides, then links out to trusted stores so you can check the current price and buy wherever you prefer.

What we do

  • Maintain over 1,000 camping and outdoor products across the major categories
  • Show a reference price and multiple store options for every product
  • Publish practical buying guides written for real Australian camping conditions
  • Keep our affiliate relationships transparent with clear disclosure on every page

How we stay independent

Retailers and affiliate programs do not pay to change our rankings or opinions. When you buy through some of our outbound links we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. That commission helps keep CampMate running and lets us expand our guides and comparisons. You can read the details on our affiliate disclosure page.

Have a question, correction or partnership idea? Please visit our contact page - we would love to hear from you.
