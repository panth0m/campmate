About CampMate

We help campers compare first and buy smarter.

CampMate Australia is a camping gear comparison website built to help outdoor enthusiasts find the right gear at the right price. We compare popular products across categories such as tents, chairs, coolers, stoves, lanterns and sleep systems.

Our goal is simple: make it easier for Australian campers to research products, compare options and click through to trusted retailers.
